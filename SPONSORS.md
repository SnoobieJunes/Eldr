# Sponsor Eldr

Eldr is an open-source, **post-quantum, end-to-end-encrypted** messenger and protocol for
the AI age — where your AI can be a real, cryptographically-labeled participant in a
conversation, and every message still stays private. It's built and maintained by one
person (plus an AI pair), given away under open licenses (Apache-2.0 packages / AGPL apps),
and it stores nothing about you.

If that's a future you want to exist, sponsoring is how you help build it.

## What your sponsorship funds — in honest priority order

1. **An independent security audit.** Eldr's own [threat model](docs/THREAT_MODEL.md) says a
   third-party audit is a precondition for any non-demo deployment, and auditing cryptographic
   software costs real money. This is the highest-impact thing sponsorship can buy: turning
   "the crypto is real and tested" into "real, tested, *and independently reviewed*."
2. **Upstream protocol work** — finishing and submitting the PQRC NIP so the confidentiality
   layer Nostr DMs lack becomes an open standard anyone can adopt.
3. **Keeping it running** — anchor-relay infrastructure and ongoing maintenance.
4. **Time** — so the work continues instead of stalling.

## What it does *not* buy

Sponsorship supports the open project. It doesn't buy private features, priority support, or
influence over the privacy-first design (SPEC §0 — privacy is the one rule that never bends).
Everything funded here lands in the open repo, for everyone.
