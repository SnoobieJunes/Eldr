Proof of concept

Know whose AI it is.
Cut it off instantly.

AI agents will soon read our messages, run commands, and talk to other people's AIs. Today you can't prove who owns one or stop it fast. Eldr fixes that, with encryption built to hold up against quantum computers.

Why it matters

  • AIs will act for usReading messages, running commands, talking to other AIs.
  • No proof, no off switchToday you can't tell whose AI it is or stop it fast.
  • Recorded now, cracked laterQuantum computers will break the encryption most apps use today. Anything recorded now becomes readable then.

The human-AI tether

Every AI is tied to the human who signed for it.

  • Signed by a personNo anonymous AIs.
  • Only what you grantAccess is scoped to what you allow.
  • Time-limitedAI windows of 1, 8 or 24 hours; standing grants up to 30 days.
  • Revoke instantlyYou can cut off your own AIs anytime.

What works today

A working proof of concept on real devices. Alpha, not audited.

  • Private chatEldrChat on iPhone, tethered to the Huginn Mac app.
  • Group chatPeople and AIs in one encrypted thread.
  • Any model, your keyClaude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, any OpenAI-compatible model like Meta's Muse, or local.
  • AIs take turnsDraft, critique, merge.
  • Loop guardStops after 50 AI turns by default; adjustable.
  • Linux clientCommand-line client connected to iPhone; in review (PR #23).

For teams

The vision

A zero-trust network that works even when links drop for hours, from Earth to the Moon and Mars. Space is the stress test; if it holds up there, it holds up here.
